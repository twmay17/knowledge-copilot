# NestArc Go Synthetic Durability Summary

This fictional engineering evaluation used three launch-version samples. All three zipper samples
completed 20,000 open-close cycles without functional failure. The three loaded seam tests failed
at 17.6, 18.1, and 18.3 kilograms, for an observed average of 18.0 kilograms. These engineering
checks are not a regulatory certification and do not establish child safety, food-contact safety,
contamination prevention, sleep safety, or medical benefit.

The current launch-version care instruction is spot-clean only. Machine washing was not evaluated
because the removable base stiffener uses an adhesive lamination whose wash durability remains
unverified.
