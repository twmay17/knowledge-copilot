# NestArc Go Product Brief

NestArc Go is a fictional modular insert that organizes an adult-carried diaper bag. The launch
version uses a recycled-PET outer shell, a thermoplastic-polyurethane wipe-clean lining, and
removable polypropylene dividers. It measures 31 by 22 by 12 centimeters and weighs 420 grams when
empty. It is an organizer, not a sleep product, child restraint, toy, feeding surface, or medical
device.

The target direct-to-consumer MSRP is $68 for the launch version. The pilot-version pitch deck used
the phrase "machine washable," but the launch care instructions remain spot-clean only while the
team evaluates how repeated wash cycles affect the removable base stiffener.

The launch target is Q2 2027 in slate and moss colors. The committed launch scope includes the base
insert, six removable dividers, and two labeled wet-item pouches. An insulated bottle pod is a
research item, not a launch commitment. No companion mobile app is planned.
