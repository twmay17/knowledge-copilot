# NestArc Go Synthetic Caregiver Research

Fifty fictional U.S. caregivers reviewed static concepts in a constructed research exercise. Thirty-six
of fifty preferred the modular-divider layout over a single-cavity control, and thirty-one of fifty
ranked fast retrieval of wipes and spare clothes among their top three organizer needs. This was a
small directional sample, not a representative market survey, and no participant used a production
unit.

At a shown price of $68, twenty-one of fifty participants selected "too expensive," nineteen selected
"reasonable," and ten selected "good value." The research supports treating price resistance as a
material objection; it does not prove purchase intent or demand at scale.
