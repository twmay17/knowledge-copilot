# NestArc Go Synthetic Commercial Notes

The fictional founder memo describes a $2.4 billion U.S. opportunity by counting all diaper bags,
organizers, and travel accessories. The fictional research note estimates $1.1 billion when limited
to diaper-bag organizers and inserts. The two figures use different category definitions and should
not be presented as one agreed market size.

The working launch assumption is direct-to-consumer distribution at a $68 MSRP. Retail distribution,
wholesale pricing, unit cost, gross margin, and international availability are not approved in this
corpus.
